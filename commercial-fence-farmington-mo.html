Commercial Security Fence FAQ

Common questions about our commercial security fence service.

How much does commercial security fence cost in Missouri?

Commercial security fencing runs $30-60/LF depending on specs (height, mesh gauge, barbed wire, automated gates). We provide insurance-compliant quotes after your free site survey.

Do you handle automated gate systems?

Yes. We install automated slide gates, swing gates, and barrier arms. Integrated with your access control (keypad, card reader, RFID, phone entry, or guard-controlled).

Can you meet our insurance requirements?

Yes. We work with insurance specs regularly — minimum height, mesh gauge, post depth, gate specs. Send us your requirements and we'll spec the fence.

Do you handle government/bonded projects?

Yes. We're licensed, insured, and bonded. We've handled Amazon DC-scale projects and municipal work. References available.

What about farms and ranches?

Farm fencing is one of our specialties. We do barbed wire, woven wire, high-tensile electric, board fence, and gates — for cattle, horses, and property lines. Farm-specific installation methods that handle Missouri terrain.
