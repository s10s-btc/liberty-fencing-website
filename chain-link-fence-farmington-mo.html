Chain-Link Fence FAQ

Common questions about our chain-link fence service.

How much does chain-link fence cost in Missouri?

Galvanized chain-link starts at $22/LF, black vinyl-coated at $24/LF. Single walk gates add $700, double drive gates $1,400. See your exact price by drawing your fence in our satellite-map designer — no phone call required.

How long does chain-link installation take?

Most residential chain-link jobs (200-300 LF) are completed in 2-4 days on your property. Commercial jobs vary — we give you a specific timeline at your free on-site walkthrough.

Do you install chain-link without digging?

Yes. We use the Rhino Multi-Pro GPD-45 gas-powered post driver to drive posts directly into the ground — no holes, no concrete, no torn-up yard. Faster install, cleaner job site.

What's the difference between galvanized and black vinyl chain-link?

Galvanized is the classic silver zinc-coated finish — most affordable, lasts 20+ years. Black vinyl-coated has a polymer coating over the galvanized steel — same durability with a residential-friendly look that blends into landscaping.

Can you match an existing chain-link fence?

Usually yes. We can match height, mesh size, and gauge. Bring photos of your existing fence to the walkthrough and we'll spec a match.
