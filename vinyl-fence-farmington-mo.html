Vinyl Privacy Fence FAQ

Common questions about our vinyl privacy fence service.

How much does vinyl privacy fence cost in Missouri?

Premium white vinyl privacy starts at $87/LF. Single walk gates add $1,100, double drive gates $2,200. See your exact price by drawing your fence in our satellite-map designer.

Does vinyl fencing yellow or fade over time?

Modern premium vinyl has UV inhibitors that prevent yellowing. We use commercial-grade vinyl with a lifetime fade warranty. After 10+ years it may have very slight fade but stays white-cream.

How long does vinyl fence last?

Lifetime (30+ years) for premium vinyl. The PVC doesn't rot, doesn't rust, doesn't splinter. Posts are the mechanical limit — and we drive them 30" deep so they last.

Can vinyl fence break in cold weather?

Quality vinyl handles Missouri winters fine (-20°F to 100°F). Cheap vinyl gets brittle. We only use commercial-grade with cold-impact modifiers.

Is vinyl fence better than wood?

Depends. Vinyl is more expensive upfront but zero maintenance. Wood is cheaper but needs staining every 2-3 years. Over 20 years, vinyl often wins on total cost of ownership.
