Common questions

Straight answers — no sales script.

How long does fence installation take?

Most residential jobs are wrapped up in 2-4 days on your property. Engine-driven posts (Rhino Multi-Pro GPD-45) mean faster installation than hand-driven crews. We'll give you a specific timeline at your free on-site walkthrough.

Do you publish your prices? Why?

Yes. We publish our per-foot prices because you deserve to know the number before we ever knock on your door. Our fence designer shows live pricing as you draw — no phone call required to get a ballpark. Final pricing confirmed at the on-site walkthrough.

What types of fences do you install?

Wood privacy (treated pine and cedar), vinyl privacy, chain-link (galvanized and black), aluminum ornamental (pool-code), and aluminum ornamental fencing. We also install gates — single walk, double drive, automated, and security. If you don't see it listed, just ask.

What's your warranty?

Workmanship warranty. If our crew's installation fails, we come back and fix it. Period. Materials carry their own manufacturer warranties (varies by material). We tell you exactly what's covered — no fine print.

Do you accept Bitcoin?

Yes. We accept Bitcoin via the Lightning Network (instant) and on-chain (any wallet). Your statement will show "Bitcoin Ranch LLC" — that's us. We also accept card, check, and cash.

What's your service area?

Farmington (home base) and a 60-mile radius covering all of Southeast Missouri: Cape Girardeau, Jackson, Sikeston, Poplar Bluff, Perryville, Dexter, Fredericktown, Ste. Genevieve, Park Hills, and more. If you're not sure you're in range, just call.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Liberty Fencing is fully licensed and insured in Missouri. We're owner-operated — when you call, you get Ryan Cooper or Scott Parsons, not a call center.

How do I get started?

Three ways: (1) Use our fence designer to draw your fence and see an instant price. (2) Call us at 573-222-0021 — Ryan or Scott will pick up personally. (3) Submit a quote request and we'll call you back within one business day. The on-site walkthrough is free.
