Wood Privacy Fence FAQ

Common questions about our wood privacy fence service.

How much does wood privacy fence cost in Missouri?

Treated pine starts at $32/LF, cedar at $42/LF. Single walk gates add $700, double drive gates $1,400. See your exact price by drawing your fence in our satellite-map designer — no phone call required to get a ballpark.

How long does a wood privacy fence last?

Pressure-treated pine lasts 20-30 years with minimal maintenance. Western Red Cedar lasts 30+ years naturally. Both handle Missouri freeze-thaw cycles well when installed correctly (posts below frost line, proper drainage).

Do you stain or seal the fence after install?

We can apply a weather-protectant stain at install (additional cost, recommended for cedar). Otherwise, we recommend staining/painting within 60 days of install for maximum lifespan.

Treated pine or cedar — which should I choose?

Treated pine is more affordable (about 25% less) and has a longer warranty against rot/insects. Cedar is naturally rot-resistant, has a richer color, and weathers to a beautiful silver-gray if left unstained.

Can you build custom heights and styles?

Yes. Standard is 6ft. We also do 4ft, 5ft, 8ft for residential and commercial. Custom styles include board-on-board, shadowbox, standard dog-ear, French gothic — just ask at the walkthrough.
