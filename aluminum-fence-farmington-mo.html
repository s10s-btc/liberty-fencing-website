Aluminum Ornamental Fence FAQ

Common questions about our aluminum ornamental fence service.

How much does aluminum ornamental fence cost in Missouri?

Ornamental aluminum starts at $59/LF. Single walk gates add $1,350, double drive gates $2,700. See your exact price by drawing your fence in our satellite-map designer.

Is aluminum fence pool code compliant?

Yes — our aluminum ornamental fence is specifically designed to meet Missouri pool safety code (4-foot minimum height, 45° or less picket spacing, self-closing gates).

Does aluminum fence rust?

No. Powder-coated aluminum doesn't rust, doesn't corrode, and handles Missouri weather perfectly. After 20 years it looks the same as the day it was installed.

Aluminum vs wrought iron — what's the difference?

Aluminum is lighter, doesn't rust, and is much cheaper than wrought iron. Wrought iron is heavier and more "old-world" looking but requires rust maintenance. Modern aluminum ornamental looks nearly identical to wrought iron at 1/3 the cost.

Can you match my house color?

Yes. We can custom-order aluminum in RAL colors to match your siding, shutters, or trim. Standard colors are black, bronze, white, and hunter green.
